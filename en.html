How an edition works

  1. Challenge callOrganizations and professionals submit problems that can be solved using open data.
  2. Challenges publishedSelected challenges are published with their data and a contact person.
  3. Hacker registrationRegister individually; teams form at the start of the event.
  4. Pre-hackathonOn Discord: an app development workshop, the code of conduct and event format, a happy hour and speed dating to form teams.
  5. HackathonThree days online with mentoring, talks and virtual laboratory visits.
  6. Judging and prizesA jury evaluates the solutions and teams receive prizes.

Extended deadlines: challenges until October 31 and registration until November 14. The campaign artwork retains the original deadlines and Spanish text.

Challenge proposers

If you have open data, or an idea of how a dataset could advance science or education, propose a challenge. Your organization gains visibility among young talent across Latin America, receives different approaches to a solution and joins the jury for its challenge. Data must be openly accessible. Challenges are selected for relevance, impact, originality, technical feasibility and clarity. Proposers are expected to participate during the hackathon.

Propose a challenge

Unsure whether your idea would make a good challenge? Ask on Discord before filling in the form.

Hackers

Open to enrolled university students living in Latin America, at undergraduate, master’s or doctoral level, in any discipline. You do not need a team beforehand: registration is individual and multidisciplinary teams form at the start of the event. A support fund is available for connectivity, expenses or other barriers to participation; apply through the same registration form. There will be prizes.

Sponsors

CoAfina is supported by scientific institutions, academic networks and companies through prizes, computing credits, data access, mentors, jury members or talks from their laboratories. In return, sponsors receive visibility on the website and in communications from CoAfina, EL-BONGÓ physics, Creative Commons Venezuela and RedCLARA, direct contact with students across the region and the opportunity to propose a challenge.

Talk to the committee on Discord

Supporting the 2026 edition: Wikimedia CH (main sponsor), INAIT and Software Sustainability Institute, alongside RedCLARA, Creative Commons Venezuela and EL-BONGÓ physics. Previous editions: ICTP / Physics Without Frontiers, Open Research Funders Group and Open Life Science.
